# Yuexu Commercialization Plan

> [中文](商业化方案.md)

## Product positioning

Yuexu is a low-distraction desktop calendar for Windows users. Its basic
experience is "the full year is visible, today is clear, and the computer stays
unburdened." It does not compete with tasks, schedules, weather, or ads for
attention.

## Editions for sale

| Edition | Suggested price | What is delivered |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Yuexu Basic | Free / open source | The repository's dark and light themes, full-year lunar calendar, and daily updates |
| Yuexu Theme Pack | CNY 12-29 | Newly designed licensed themes, solar-term illustrations, and screen-ratio presets |
| Yuexu Pro | CNY 39-59 one-time | Per-monitor layouts, holiday markers, font and icon safe areas, and brand-color import |
| Team deployment | Priced per device | Signed installer, silent deployment, enterprise themes, and priority support |

The open-source basic edition and paid delivery do not conflict. Users buy
design, signed releases, reliable updates, deployment capabilities, and support,
not restrictions on source code.

## Launch channels

1. GitHub Release: establish trust and provide checksums and a feedback channel.
2. Xiaohongshu, Jike, and SSPAI: show real 4K dark and light theme captures,
   emphasizing low resource use and readable lunar-calendar information.
3. Aifadian, WeChat Store, and digital-goods platforms: sell theme packs and Pro
   licenses.
4. WeCom / Feishu ecosystems: provide custom wallpaper for enterprise
   administration and design teams.

## Required before launch

- Apply for a Windows code-signing certificate to remove the unknown-publisher
  warning.
- Prepare screenshots for 16:9, 16:10, and ultrawide displays, plus a 30-second
  demonstration video.
- Establish purchase, refund, theme-pack licensing, and privacy notice pages.
- Use an explicit license or account-based delivery for Pro features; do not add
  backdoors or telemetry to the basic edition.
